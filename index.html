How we choose

Every bottle starts with the farm

We look for growers who work with the land, and makers who work at a human scale.

  • Organic vineyards

    No synthetic pesticides or herbicides in the vineyard, so the wine tastes like the place it grew.

  • Biodynamic farms

    Farms that follow the planting calendar and treat the soil as something alive.

  • Small producers

    Family cellars and small estates, often one hillside and a handful of labels.

  • Hidden gems

    Bottles you will not find in every shop, from regions worth getting to know.
