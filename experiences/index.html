Pretty in Pink

Four Rosé Wines · From Corsica to Beaujolais

$120 the case

View the case
  1. 01

    Terra Vecchia GR20 Rosé

    IGP Île de Beauté · Corsica, France · Niellucciu & Sciaccarellu

    Named for Europe’s most demanding hiking trail — and it earns it. Pale salmon-copper in the glass with a nose of red rhubarb, white peach and green pear edged with white pepper. This is what a Corsican morning smells like.

  2. 02

    Umathum Rosa Rosé Saignée

    Burgenland, Austria · Zweigelt, Sankt Laurent & Blaufränkisch · Biodynamic

    Made by the traditional saignée method — red grapes macerated on their skins before fermentation to pull colour, aroma and soft tannin from the berries. The result is a rosé with genuine depth and a red-wine backbone.

  3. 03

    Diwald Zweigelt Rosé

    Wagram, Austria · Zweigelt & Pinot Noir · Organic since 1976

    Diwald set out to make a serious rosé, not sugar water — and they’ve delivered. Part saignée, part whole-bunch pressed from cooler sites, with spontaneous fermentation in tank. Zweigelt gives the fruit, Pinot Noir gives the tension.

  4. 04

    Croifolie Rosé Labise

    AOP Beaujolais-Villages · Beaujolais, France · 100% Gamay · Guide Hachette 2024

    Four generations of the same family farming 6 hectares in Salles-Arbuissonnas — Gamay from clay-granitic soils, harvested by hand and aged on fine lees for 2–3 months before bottling.

Viva Italia

Four Italian Wines · From Veneto to Tuscany

$105 the case

View the case
  1. 01

    Cantina del Garda Soave Classico

    DOC Soave Classico · Veneto, Italy · 100% Garganega · 91pts Falstaff

    Grown on volcanic hillside soils in the Classico zone — the heartland of Soave — and aged six months in steel to preserve every bit of freshness the terroir gives. Pale yellow with green-gold reflections in the glass.

  2. 02

    Alessandro Rivetto Langhe Nascetta Albori

    DOC Langhe · Piedmont, Italy · 100% Nascetta · Hand Harvested

    Nascetta is one of Piedmont’s rarest indigenous white grapes — nearly extinct until a handful of producers in the Langhe brought it back. Rivetto is one of the best. Brilliant straw-yellow, hand-harvested, fermented slow and cold at 15–18°C.

  3. 03

    Menicucci Chianti DOCG

    DOCG Chianti · Tuscany, Italy · Sangiovese, Cabernet Sauvignon & Merlot

    From the rich soils of Cerreto and Montalbano in Tuscany — Sangiovese doing the heavy lifting at 90%, with Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot rounding out the edges. Hand-selected, gently pressed, fermented at controlled temperatures for 10 days.

  4. 04

    Benedetti Valpolicella Superiore Ripasso Classico

    DOC Valpolicella · Veneto, Italy · Corvina, Corvinone, Rondinella & Molinara · 14.5%

    Ripasso is one of Italy’s great winemaking tricks: take a good Valpolicella and referment it on the leftover skins of Amarone. You get deeper colour, richer aromatics and more alcohol without the price tag of the Amarone itself.

Weeknight Pours

Two Whites, Two Reds · France & Italy

$75 the case

View the case
  1. 01

    Cellier des Demoiselles Chardonnay

    IGP Pays d’Oc · Languedoc, France · 100% Chardonnay · Machine Harvested at Night

    Chardonnay grown on clay-limestone with a limestone glaze in the south of France — picked at night to preserve freshness, then fermented clean in steel. Pale in colour with green highlights and an expressive nose of grapefruit, acidulous notes and a touch of white fruit.

  2. 02

    Tenimenti Civa Pinot Grigio Ronc Laris

    DOC Friuli · Friuli Colli Orientali, Italy · 100% Pinot Grigio · “Ponca” Soils

    Grown on the ancient marl and sandstone “Ponca” soils of Friuli Colli Orientali — one of Italy’s best addresses for white wine. Harvested in the cool of early September morning, soft pressed, fermented at 16–18°C for 15 days.

  3. 03

    Terra Vallona IGP Comte Tolosan Rouge

    IGP Comte Tolosan · South West France · Merlot & Cabernets · 14%

    From Cave du Marmandais in the heart of the French South West — a co-operative with deep roots in the region and a straightforward philosophy: make wines that are genuinely enjoyable without overthinking them.

  4. 04

    Menicucci Montepulciano d’Abruzzo

    DOC Montepulciano d’Abruzzo · Abruzzo, Italy · 100% Montepulciano · 13.5%

    Montepulciano d’Abruzzo is one of Italy’s most reliable red grapes — deep colour, generous fruit, soft tannins, and a price that never makes you wince. Menicucci harvest mid-September, macerate for 7–8 days for colour and structure, then age three months on fine lees.
