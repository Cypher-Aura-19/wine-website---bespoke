The producer

Bella Glamour 0.0 is a light toast drink. It has a fresh, delicate flavor that is completed by the liveliness of carbon dioxide which develops a fine and persistent perlage.

Characterized by intense aromas of white fruit, including pear, apple, and peach, floral notes of freshly picked flowers, and acacia flowers. Low calorie and rigorously 0% alcohol for a healthy, natural pleasure.

Bella is an Italian fashion alcohol free drink, crafted to charm the most exigent and curious palates all over the world. Made by a family winery half a century old, with exquisite local grapes from Northern Italy, to appeal a unique community of lifestylers always looking for new amazing tasting experiences.

Vermouth Zero BELLA is made according to an old recipe from the Veneto region, the land of great wines. The aroma is warm with notes of various botanicals, including cinchona bark, bitter orange, gentian and vanilla. On the palate it is soft and full, the balance between sugar and aromatic notes is pleasantly perceived, with a slightly bitter finish.
