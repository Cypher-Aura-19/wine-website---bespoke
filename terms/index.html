Common questions

How can I pay for the products?
You can use the credit card method provided when you check out.
How does delivery work?
We deliver throughout the province of Ontario, and deliveries take place between 9am and 5pm, Monday to Friday.
What does delivery cost?
Delivery is free within the GTA.
How long does it take to receive the wine once I have placed an order online?
You will receive your order in 2 to 3 business days.

Prices

Prices listed are per bottle, but must be sold by case lot, and all prices are in Canadian dollars. Due to currency fluctuations and LCBO policy changes, prices are subject to change without notice.

For your convenience, prices include:

  • Export price
  • Freight
  • Customs charges
  • Duties
  • Excise
  • LCBO markup of 64%
  • LCBO wine levy
  • LCBO environmental fee
  • Agency fee
  • Gallonage fee, where applicable

Prices do not include HST and bottle deposit.

Buying through the LCBO

Bespoke Wine & Spirits is not permitted to sell beverage alcohol, but we facilitate an arrangement whereby you can purchase directly from the LCBO.

The LCBO’s price for the products is available in advance from Bespoke Wine & Spirits upon request.

Cases

Wine may only be purchased in case lots. There are twelve bottles in a case unless otherwise noted.

Orders and delivery

The LCBO requires 36 hours to process an order. Please expect a minimum of two business days before receiving your order. All rush orders will be subject to a surcharge.

We deliver five days a week in the Toronto area, and to most parts of central Ontario on a weekly basis.

Damage and returns

Loss or damage of goods should be notified to Bespoke Wine & Spirits immediately upon receipt. Failure to notify us in due time may result in denial of liability.

All products returned are subject to a 20% restocking fee.

Age

You must be 19 years of age or older to place or receive a beverage alcohol order.
