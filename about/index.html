Wine & Spirits Agency

Bespoke wines for your bespoke moments

An Ontario wine agency since 2017. We bring bottles you won't find on LCBO shelves to your table.

Our story

With more than twenty-five years of combined experience, our team looks for exceptional wines from small producers and brings these hidden gems to your table. Every bottle has its own story and its own flavour, and every one is chosen by hand.

What we stand for

  1. 01

    Hidden gems, not shelf staples

    We curate exceptional wines that are not found in local LCBO stores, celebrating the craft of the people who make them.

  2. 02

    Your palate comes first

    Every palate is different, so we tailor our list to you. Seasoned connoisseur or curious beginner, you get recommendations made for you.

  3. 03

    Producers who think like us

    We champion winemakers devoted to the excellence and artistry of their wines, who put quality before the bottom line.

A portfolio built bottle by bottle, directly with the families who make it.

Co-founder1 / 3

Frank Bruno

With over twenty years of experience navigating Ontario's alcohol import scene, Frank has honed an unparalleled expertise by tasting thousands of wines. Alongside his business partner Matt Wolman, he takes personal charge of wine procurement at Bespoke, engaging directly with winery owners to curate products that meet our standards.

His commitment to integrity and transparency guides all his business dealings. His work within Ontario's restaurant sector has built many of the relationships the business stands on.

Outside work, Frank is devoted to his family, to travel, and to his dog, Tommy Boy.

Co-founder2 / 3

Matt Wolman

After working his way up in many of Toronto's finest restaurants, in both front and back of house, Matt was looking for a new challenge. With his wine knowledge and natural sales ability, wine and spirit sales was the perfect fit.

After excelling with several distinguished agencies, he met his now partner Frank Bruno. From their personal connection, shared passion for wine and like-minded business sense, Bespoke Wine and Spirits was established in 2017.

Matt's greatest passions are growing the business with Frank and raising his young family.

Sales lead3 / 3

Natasha Mok

Natasha joined Bespoke in 2019 as an Account Rep for high-end restaurants and golf clubs. As Bespoke grew, she took on the sales team, working with licensee accounts and winemakers.

Growing up in her parents' restaurant gave her a love of hospitality, and a career in finance sharpened it. She enjoys the whole wine selection process and understands what clients and the sales team each need.

Outside work, she spends time with her two teenage sons, travels, sees friends, and of course enjoys good wine.
