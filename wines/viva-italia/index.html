In the case

Four Italian Wines · From Veneto to Tuscany

  1. 01

    Cantina del Garda Soave Classico

    DOC Soave Classico · Veneto, Italy · 100% Garganega · 91pts Falstaff

    Grown on volcanic hillside soils in the Classico zone — the heartland of Soave — and aged six months in steel to preserve every bit of freshness the terroir gives. Pale yellow with green-gold reflections in the glass.

  2. 02

    Alessandro Rivetto Langhe Nascetta Albori

    DOC Langhe · Piedmont, Italy · 100% Nascetta · Hand Harvested

    Nascetta is one of Piedmont’s rarest indigenous white grapes — nearly extinct until a handful of producers in the Langhe brought it back. Rivetto is one of the best. Brilliant straw-yellow, hand-harvested, fermented slow and cold at 15–18°C.

  3. 03

    Menicucci Chianti DOCG

    DOCG Chianti · Tuscany, Italy · Sangiovese, Cabernet Sauvignon & Merlot

    From the rich soils of Cerreto and Montalbano in Tuscany — Sangiovese doing the heavy lifting at 90%, with Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot rounding out the edges. Hand-selected, gently pressed, fermented at controlled temperatures for 10 days.

  4. 04

    Benedetti Valpolicella Superiore Ripasso Classico

    DOC Valpolicella · Veneto, Italy · Corvina, Corvinone, Rondinella & Molinara · 14.5%

    Ripasso is one of Italy’s great winemaking tricks: take a good Valpolicella and referment it on the leftover skins of Amarone. You get deeper colour, richer aromatics and more alcohol without the price tag of the Amarone itself.
