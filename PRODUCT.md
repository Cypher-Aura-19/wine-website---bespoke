# Bespoke Wine & Spirits

## Product
A static redesign of bespokewineandspirits.com, the site of an Ontario wine and spirits agency (founded 2017, Vaughan, ON). It presents the portfolio and producers; the cart and checkout remain on the existing Shopify store (bespokewineandspirits.com), which the static pages hand off to.

## Users and jobs
- Ontario wine buyers looking for bottles they won't find on LCBO shelves: browse, understand a wine, buy by the case.
- Restaurants and trade buyers building a list: find producers, ask for recommendations, contact the team.

## Position
Hidden gems over shelf staples: small, often organic and biodynamic producers (35 producers in 8 countries on the producers page), chosen by hand, with recommendations made for the buyer's palate.

## Product facts to preserve
- Sold mostly by the case (12 or 6 bottles); the store shows a per-bottle price and the case price. Some items are single units.
- Delivery throughout Ontario, Mon–Fri 9am–5pm; free within the GTA; 2–3 business days. Payment by credit card at checkout.
- Contact: 647-286-1153; 131 Whitmore Rd., Unit 19, Vaughan, ON L4L 6E4; Instagram and Facebook.
- Product data (title, producer, type, grape, country, region, vintage, bottle size, bottles per case, price, stock, spec sheet) comes from the Shopify store; nothing about a wine is invented.

## Brand commitments
- Cream ground (#fcf4ec), muted red type (#8a3d3d), Coconat display over Lexend Deca, square corners, hairlines.
- Motion: GSAP, ScrollTrigger, SplitText, Lenis smooth scroll; page-turn panel between pages; reduced motion respected.
- Refine approved designs rather than replacing them; no text laid over busy photographs.

## Platform
web

## Open decisions
- No tasting notes or food pairings exist in the store data; do not author them unless the team supplies them.
